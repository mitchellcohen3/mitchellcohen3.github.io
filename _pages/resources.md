A collection of textbooks, papers, and tutorials that I've found useful during my PhD.

## Textbooks
- [**State Estimation for Robotics**](https://asrl.utias.utoronto.ca/~tdb/bib/barfoot_ser24.pdf) - Timothy D. Barfoot. Covers fundementals of probabilistic state estimation, 
- [**Probabilistic Robotics**](https://mitpress.mit.edu/9780262201629/probabilistic-robotics/) - Thrun, Burgard, and Fox. 
- [**Factor Graphs for Robot Perception**](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~kaess/pub/Dellaert17fnt.pdf) - Dellaert and Kaess. 
- [**Bayesian Filtering and Smoothing**](https://users.aalto.fi/~ssarkka/pub/cup_book_online_20131111.pdf) - Simo S&auml;rkk&auml;.
- [**Convex Optimization**](https://web.stanford.edu/~boyd/cvxbook/) - Boyd and Vandenberghe. Free PDF, lecture videos, and slides.
- [**Numerical Optimization**](https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-0-387-40065-5) - Nocedal and Wright. Standard reference for Newton, quasi-Newton, and trust-region methods.

## Lie Groups
- [**A Micro Lie Theory for State Estimation in Robotics**](https://arxiv.org/abs/1812.01537) - Sol&agrave;, Deray, and Atchuthan. Most approachable entry point to Lie groups
- [**Quaternion Kinematics for the Error-State Kalman Filter**](https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.02508) - Joan Sol&agrave;. Especially useful for quaternion math.
- [**Lie Groups for Computer Vision**](https://www.ethaneade.org/lie_groups.pdf) - Ethan Eade. Great resource for the closed form expressions for expressions involving common Lie groups.

## Computer Vision
- [**Multiple View Geometry in Computer Vision**](https://www.robots.ox.ac.uk/~vgg/hzbook/) - Hartley and Zisserman. Standard reference for projective geometry and for reconstructing 3D structure from 2D images.

## Visual-Inertial Navigation
- [**A Multi-State Constraint Kalman Filter for Vision-Aided Inertial Navigation**](https://intra.ece.ucr.edu/~mourikis/papers/MourikisRoumeliotis-ICRA07.pdf) - Mourikis and Roumeliotis. The original MSCKF paper.
- [**On-Manifold Preintegration for Real-Time Visual-Inertial Odometry**](https://arxiv.org/abs/1512.02363) - Forster, Carlone, Dellaert, and Scaramuzza. IMU preintegration and factor-graph based visual-inertial odometry.
- [**Visual-Inertial Navigation: A Concise Review**](https://arxiv.org/abs/1906.02650) - Guoquan Huang. Great survey paper and overview to the field.
- [**OpenVINS documentation**](https://docs.openvins.com/) - Lots of derivations of some of the key parts of MSCKF-based VIO, including propagation, feature representations, and delayed initialization.
- [**IMU Noise Model - Kalibr**](https://github.com/ethz-asl/kalibr/wiki/IMU-Noise-Model) - Explanation of the typical IMU noise model assumed in visual-inertial navigation, with references on how to compute IMU noise parameters using Allan Variance.

## Visual Odometry and SLAM
- [**Visual Odometry - Part I: The First 30 Years and Fundementals**](https://rpg.ifi.uzh.ch/docs/VO_Part_I_Scaramuzza.pdf) - tutorial-style overview on visual odometry. Useful for understanding the problem formulation at a high level, and contains useful discussions on visual odometry vs visual SLAM.


## Courses/Lectures
- [**Cyrill Stachniss Youtube Lectures**](https://www.youtube.com/c/CyrillStachniss) - Full lectures on common robotics topics, including calibration, localization, mapping. The "5 Minutes with Cyril" series has approachable high-level introductions to many robotics topics.

## C++
- [**learncpp.com**](https://www.learncpp.com/) - Free tutorials and lessons covering many of the fundamentals of C++, very accessible and well written.
- [**A Tour of C++**](https://www.stroustrup.com/tour3.html) - Bjarne Stroustrup 
- [**Effective Modern C++**](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/effective-modern-c/9781491908419/) - Scott Meyers.
- [**C++ Core Guidelines**](https://isocpp.github.io/CppCoreGuidelines/CppCoreGuidelines) - Stroustrup and Sutter.
- [**cppreference.com**](https://en.cppreference.com/) - Reference for the language.
- [**Awesome Modern C++**](https://github.com/rigtorp/awesome-modern-cpp) - A list of C++ libraries, tools, and resources.
- [**Code for Yourself**](https://www.youtube.com/code-for-yourself) - Collection of tutorials from someone with a career in robotics and computer vision, mostly related to C++ fundamentals. Also has some very useful videos on CMake and build systems.
